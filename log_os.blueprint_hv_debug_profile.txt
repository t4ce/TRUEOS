TRUEOS debug profile: blueprint-hv

Purpose:
  Follow Blueprint loading, app-vm lifecycle, x86 carrier admission, VM exits,
  and hypervisor state transitions without display/GPU log volume.

Accepted areas:
  Hv        Up(Trace)
  Blueprint Up(Trace)
  everything else Up(Warn)

Suppressed noisy paths:
  Gfx/Gpgpu/Render Info+ records
  Intel render/display NGIN diagnostics
  Shell2 render sampling
  QuadTexture diagnostics

Enablement is compile-time in src/log_os.rs. The boot record identifies this
profile as profile=blueprint-hv-debug=1.
