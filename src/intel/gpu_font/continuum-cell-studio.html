Known glyphs have their own constructors
Auto routes the microfont box-drawing characters, generated Braille, and Block Elements to cell-aware construction. Box arms are declared north/east/south/west ports. Double rails are the Boolean band around a channel, not crossing blobs. Virtual sites complete declared terminals; Border-site repair instead requires actual inward lattice evidence. Braille decodes occupied bit positions, uses independent parametric dots or capsules, and only joins column runs when explicitly enabled. Blocks use exact fractions or the crate's rounded full-cell coverage.
Box and block regions are triangulated as a global rectangle arrangement. Shared ports weld across cell seams. Point-only contacts become separate mesh sheets. Braille curves are polygonal approximations controlled by curve detail. Mixed text containing letters uses the general field; the automatic cell route accepts box, block, Braille, and blank cells.
Bias in the general field
The crossfader attenuates links in the competing direction. Leave-one-out continuation evidence protects supported strokes; unsupported long gaps are penalized. Neutral density balancing happens before attenuation so it cannot undo the bias. This is a tunable heuristic, not glyph recognition.
Two shape families; one mesh interface
Both constructors output positions and indexed triangles. The optional WebGL2 backend uploads and draws those buffers with a simple vertex/fragment shader pair. It does not infer the glyph in a fragment shader. The Canvas vector-path fallback uses the same mesh boundary loops. GPU availability is reported in the renderer control.

01 · Convolved membrane — the starting point
Every accepted link contributes along its entire length, not just at its endpoints. Numerical integration of a compact Gaussian around that skeleton creates a shared field. Endpoint caps keep isolated sites and terminals alive. A local mass-compensation heuristic reduces inflated junctions.
F(x) = Σ qᵢ Kσ(x − pᵢ)
       + Σ wₑ ∫ₑ Kσ(x − s) ds
The integral is approximated by weighted quadrature. Dividing a link into more samples does not increase its total mass. Site and link weights are scaled locally when Density balance is enabled.
02 · Charge bath — point potential
Only radial source kernels contribute. Overlapping influence creates necks and merges islands without explicit graph links. These are modelling potentials, not Coulomb forces or charge trajectories.
03 · Directional tissue — local orientation
A local covariance tensor of neighboring links estimates a principal direction. Elliptical kernels stretch along that direction in coherent regions, then their fields add together. Ambiguous junctions stay more isotropic.
04 · Distance envelope — geometric baseline
The maximum of point and segment kernels gives a rounded union at the chosen threshold. Unlike the additive fields, nearby influences do not cooperate to form soft necks. This deliberately exposes the difference between an offset union and a shared field.
The mesh is real; the field is not the final rendering
The field is sampled in source coordinates. An adaptive quadtree merges uniform interior tiles. Neighbor splits are inserted into shared edges; each leaf is triangulated and clipped against the threshold. Vertices are welded, boundaries traced, and holes retained. The body view fills those boundary loops to avoid antialiasing seams between adjacent triangles. Mesh view shows the actual indexed triangulation.
Sampling and quadrature are numerical approximations. Detail controls the geometric sampling error; zoom alone doesn't change the model. Extremely thin bridges can require higher detail. This is not constrained Delaunay triangulation or a hardware tessellation stage.
