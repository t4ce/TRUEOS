`apidemo/main.rs` stops probing the kernel-owned primary browser window, while `trueos/src/ui2.rs` and `trueos-sys/src/vcabi.rs` remove `primary_browser_window()` and `trueos_cabi_ui2_primary_browser_window_id`.
